What we do

Themes of Capacity building

Learn how we can help

AI Productivity & Workforce Enablement

Delivered a hands-on AI Productivity workshop for NBCC, covering Finance, HR, and Marketing functions. We managed the entire training lifecycle end-to-end from trainer sourcing to curriculum design and delivery, equipping teams with practical and outcome-focused skills.

Livelihood Promotion

Livelihood promotion enables individuals and communities to translate skills into sustainable economic opportunities. Through entrepreneurship development, market linkages, and enterprise support, we help create resilient livelihoods, enhance incomes, and foster long-term self-reliance.

Digital and market Enablement

This initiative manages the end-to-end digital migration of MSMEs, enabling a digital-first approach to enhance competitiveness. It provides guidance for e-commerce onboarding, including digital store creation, cataloging, and marketplace compliance. It also supports business process digitization, automated inventory tracking, and tracks technology adoption and e-commerce onboarding to measure market and technology outcomes.

Evidence-Based Capacity Building

Our evidence-based capacity-building programmes strengthen the capabilities of stakeholders across all levels, translating national priorities and SDG commitments into local action. Through blended learning, practical activities, mentoring, and digital tools, we ensure that learning is relevant, applied, and measurable. Every intervention is designed around real responsibilities and assessed against outcomes that drive sustainable impact.

Institutional Strengthening

We strengthen institutions by building the capacities of leaders and frontline functionaries and fostering systems that improve governance and service delivery. Through capacity building and exposure to proven models, we enable institutions to adopt best practices and deliver sustainable outcomes at scale.

Entrepreneurship & Enterprise Development

Entrepreneurship Development Programmes (EDPs) equip aspiring and established business owners with the skills needed to grow and scale. The programme focuses on entrepreneurial mindset development, opportunity identification, market assessment, and operational excellence, including financial planning, risk management, and leadership. Through hands-on mentoring, it helps transform ideas into sustainable, investment-ready businesses.
